Mercedes-Benz 300SL Roadster

Bespoke Editorial & Timeless Heritage Study

The Apex Series // Ref. 03

Designed as an exploration of high-key luxury minimalism. Shifting away from moody obsidian environments, this piece pairs rich espresso-metallic paintwork against warm architectural cream tones, minimalist typography, and delicate Swiss-grid framing.

Design Framework Swiss-Grid & Serif Editorial
Primary Tool Adobe Photoshop 2021

Engineering Heritage & Tone

Built around the iconic 1957 architecture of the direct-injected inline-6 platform. The typography balances classic European automotive serif italics with ultra-clean tracked metadata to achieve true hardcover lookbook presence.
