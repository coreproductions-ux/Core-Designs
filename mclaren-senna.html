McLaren Senna Edition

Swiss-Grid Graphic & Aerodynamic Layout Study

Track-Focused Composition & Geometry

Designed around the aggressive, uncompromised aerodynamic profile of the McLaren Senna. The layout emphasizes technical hierarchy, sharp framing, and deep obsidian isolation to translate raw track performance into a minimalist editorial poster.

Design Framework Swiss-Grid System
Primary Tool Adobe Photoshop 2021

Execution & Layer Masking

Advanced color grading and precise layer masking were utilized to separate the cobalt blue carbon chassis from the deep background, locking typography strictly onto mathematical grid alignments.

Visual Focus Color Grading & Layer Masking
